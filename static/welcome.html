Symphony · AI Finance Cohort

Let's build your cohort.

Symphony is a team of AI workers that operate as one organism — you direct, they coordinate the rest among themselves. You don't set them all up by hand. You start with one worker — your Team Orchestrator — and the two of you design and stand up the rest together.

01Start with your Team Orchestrator

Symphony begins with a single worker: the Team Orchestrator. It's the one who helps you decide what your cohort should do, which workers you need, and walks you through setting each one up. Everything else grows from here.

02Bring your Team Orchestrator online

Your Team Orchestrator is a Claude Code session. Open a terminal and paste the single command for your platform — one line loads your cohort's private workspace and wakes the Team Orchestrator together.

macOS — open Terminal and paste:
"$HOME/Library/Application Support/Symphony/body/symphony_wake.sh" tia
Windows — open PowerShell and paste:
& "$env:USERPROFILE\Symphony\body\symphony_wake.ps1" tia

Windows command finalizes with the Windows installer — macOS is live now.

The first time you run it, expect three quick prompts:
  1. Claude Code asks to trust the folder

    It's your own Symphony install — agree to give it access.

  2. Paste this as your first message

    This replaces typing wakeup — it tells your new worker the setup is legitimate, who you are, and what to do next. The same message wakes every worker you add later (Step 4), each one reading its own role from its config.

    Edit anything above before copying — if the name's wrong or you want to add a line, your edits come along when you copy.

    Your worker may pause once to confirm this is really you before proceeding — that's expected, not broken. Just answer plainly (yes, this is your setup) and it'll continue.

  3. Authorize at the legitimacy gate

    By design, a brand-new worker pauses to confirm it's really you before it acts as your Team Orchestrator — a file can't vouch for itself. Choose "Yes, proceed as tia." Your yes is what roots your cohort's authority (every worker you add later traces its legitimacy back to this moment).

Then it wakes up as itself. The session loads its identity, confirms it, and joins your team-room — ready to help. Use the terminal Claude Code (not the desktop app): only it inherits the private workspace the command sets up, which is what keeps your cohort walled off on your own machine.

03Design your cohort together

Open the team-room and start a conversation with your Team Orchestrator. To get a productive one going, tell it three things:

The work
What your cohort will focus on — its domain. e.g. “FP&A variance analysis for the Neuroscience P&L” or “SEC-filing research on pharma peers.”
The questions
The kinds of things you want answered day-to-day — this helps it shape the right cohort for you.
The shape
Roughly how many workers + any roles you have in mind — or just ask the Team Orchestrator to recommend a roster for your work.

A good opener: “I want a cohort that does <your work>. Help me figure out which workers I need and set them up.” From there your Team Orchestrator recommends the roles, pulls the matching archetypes, and walks you through bringing each worker online — one at a time.
