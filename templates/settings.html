Rules & overrides
Prerequisite rules (Aristotle)
Teach Jasper a gate: "don't treat X as ready to act on until Y has been seen for the same project or supplier." For example, a signature request shouldn't be flagged ready to sign until an approved purchase order has come through for that same deal. Jasper only ever says "no confirmation seen yet" — never that something hasn't happened — since it can only see what's actually arrived in your mail.
