The short version

Jasper reads your mail, works out what actually needs your attention, and answers questions about it — grounded in your real data every time, never a guess. It drafts, it doesn't send. It suggests, it doesn't approve. It never auto-deletes a real document. Everything below is either fully working today or clearly marked as not yet connected.

Start here

Ready for you this morning

The Morning Queue — a task list scored from real signals: how close a deadline is, how long a thread's gone quiet, and any dollar value mentioned in it. "Now what?" surfaces the single most pressing one. Nothing here sends or approves anything by itself.

Ask anything

Ask Jasper, in Back Office

Type a plain question into the Back Office chat without an @-mention and Jasper answers from precedent, prior synthesis, and linked evidence already in your work graph. No web search, no LLM guess — when there isn't enough grounding, it tells you that honestly instead of making something up.

Track the work

PCC and Projects views

Real issue and project tracking, built entirely from your ingested mail — not sample data. Click into any issue for the evidence behind it: which messages, what changed, and why Jasper classified it the way it did.

Learns over time

Total Recall

When you confirm or correct how Jasper classified something, it remembers — the same category-and-supplier pattern gets more confident (or less) the next time it comes up. You'll see this cited directly as "precedent" in the reasoning behind a task.

Manage

Settings

Your name, worker names, and the chat history window — plus Data & retention: how long operational logs are kept, automated database backups, and a disk-usage view. Real supplier documents are never touched by any retention setting.

Not yet connected

My Work

Visible but disabled — it needs Ariba, SAP, Aravo, ServiceNow, or ContractPodAI access this install doesn't have yet. Nothing is hidden about this; the tab itself says so.

What Jasper deliberately won't do
  • Send an email, or take any action, on its own — everything is a draft or a suggestion you review first.
  • Approve anything. Approval chains are shown for context; the decision is always yours.
  • Auto-delete a real document. Data-retention rules apply only to operational data (logs, diagnostic history) — never to supplier contracts, SOWs, or anything similar, under any setting.
  • Guess. If Jasper doesn't have enough grounded evidence to answer confidently, it says so instead of inventing an answer.
